## 📖 Introduction

Space research is expensive, slow, and painfully hard to access — real microgravity is a scarce resource, gate-kept by national space agencies and multi-year mission timelines. So scientists on Earth do the next best thing: they build machines that *fake* it. Enter the **Random Positioning Machine (RPM)** and its cousins — clinostats, RWVs, RCCS bioreactors — devices that constantly reorient a sample so gravity averages out to (roughly) zero.

Dozens of research groups worldwide have used these machines to study how human, animal, plant, and microbial cells behave when gravity stops being a constant. The problem? Their findings are scattered across 50+ papers, a dozen journals, and just as many data repositories — with no single place that pulls it all together.

**This repository is that place.**

---

## 🌌 About

This is a curated, structured, and continuously growing collection of **research papers and datasets** on RPM and simulated microgravity, built through a systematic literature review. Every entry has been read, tagged, and — where possible — traced back to its original raw dataset (gene expression, proteomics, imaging, or numerical data), so researchers, students, and reviewers can find and reuse this data without repeating the months of digging that went into finding it.

## 🔬 Our Research

This project was completed as a research internship task, split into two linked deliverables:

| | Project 1 — Research Dataset Creation | Project 2 — AI Benchmark Pipeline |
|---|---|---|
| **Goal** | Perform a systematic literature review, extract variables from published studies, standardize terminology and units, and package it as an open, structured dataset | Formulate prediction/classification problems on the curated dataset, run EDA and preprocessing, and benchmark baseline ML models |
| **Output** | This repository — v1.0 of the open dataset + a detailed data dictionary | A reproducible training pipeline (Random Forest, XGBoost, Neural Network) with benchmark results |

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary><b>📌 How do I cite this repository?</b></summary>

Please see the [Citation](#-citation) section below for BibTeX and plain-text citation formats.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>📌 How were papers selected for this review?</b></summary>

Papers were identified through a systematic literature review using **Elicit**, **Consensus**, and manual searches across PubMed, Google Scholar, and journal-specific databases. Selection criteria:

1. The study must use a **Random Positioning Machine (RPM)**, clinostat, rotating wall vessel (RWV), or RCCS bioreactor
2. The study must investigate **biological responses** to simulated microgravity (or characterize the device itself)
3. The study must be **published in a peer-reviewed journal** or available as a preprint

De-duplication and screening were managed in **Zotero**.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>📌 What does "to be added" mean in the dataset column?</b></summary>

It means we have confirmed the paper exists and is relevant, but:
- The authors did not deposit raw data in a public repository, **or**
- A public dataset may exist but we have not yet located and verified the link

We are actively working to fill in these entries. Contributions are welcome!

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>📌 How can I contribute a paper or dataset?</b></summary>

We welcome contributions! You can:
1. **Open an Issue** on this repository with the paper DOI and (if available) the dataset link
2. **Submit a Pull Request** adding a new row to the dataset table and a corresponding paper detail section
3. **Email any contributor** listed in the [Contributors](#-contributors) section

Please follow the existing format for consistency.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>📌 Can I use this dataset for my research?</b></summary>

Yes! This repository is licensed under **CC BY 4.0**. You are free to share and adapt the material for any purpose, as long as you give appropriate credit. See the [Citation](#-citation) section for how to cite.

Individual datasets linked from this repository have their own licenses — please check the original data repository for terms.

</details>
