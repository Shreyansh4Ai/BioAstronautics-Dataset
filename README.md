# Curated Datasets for RPM and Simulated Microgravity Research

## About
This repository serves as a curated collection of datasets related to Random Positioning Machine (RPM) and simulated microgravity research. The goal is to provide a structured, easily accessible resource for researchers studying the effects of simulated microgravity on biological and physical systems. The repository aggregates data from various published studies to facilitate secondary analysis and reproducibility.

## Our Research
The datasets in this repository were collected as part of a systematic literature review and data extraction project. We identified relevant studies using standardized search queries across major scientific databases (such as PubMed, Scopus, and Web of Science). 

The data was sourced in two primary ways:
1. **Public Websites**: For papers that provided their raw or supplementary data in public repositories (e.g., Figshare, Zenodo, or journal supplementary materials), we extracted and linked directly to those resources.
2. **Direct Email Requests**: For papers where the underlying numerical data was not publicly available, we directly emailed the original authors to request access to their datasets.
